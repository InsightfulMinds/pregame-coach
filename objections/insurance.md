# Insurance Objections

## 1. "I already have coverage."

**Psychology:** They believe having a policy means the problem is solved.

**Rebuttal:** "Good. Then this is not about starting over. It is about checking whether what you have still matches your life today."

## 2. "I do not want to switch agents."

**Psychology:** Loyalty and trust are protecting the current relationship.

**Rebuttal:** "I respect that. Keep a good agent if they are serving you well. My question is whether there is anything you have not been able to get answered clearly."

## 3. "Insurance is confusing."

**Psychology:** They fear making the wrong choice and being stuck with it.

**Rebuttal:** "Exactly why I do not lead with plan names. First we identify what you need protected, then we compare only the options that match that."

## 4. "I cannot afford more insurance."

**Psychology:** They hear "new bill," not "risk transfer."

**Rebuttal:** "Then we should be careful. The point is not more coverage; it is the right protection for the dollars you already have available."

## 5. "I need to ask my spouse."

**Psychology:** They need shared confidence before taking responsibility.

**Rebuttal:** "Absolutely. What would your spouse need to understand first: the cost, the coverage, or what happens if nothing changes?"

## 6. "I am healthy. I do not need this."

**Psychology:** They are judging future risk by today's condition.

**Rebuttal:** "That is the best time to look. Insurance gets harder to buy when you actually need it."

## 7. "I had a bad experience with an agent."

**Psychology:** Prior disappointment has damaged trust.

**Rebuttal:** "That would make me cautious too. What went wrong last time? I will tell you plainly whether I can solve that."

## 8. "Just send me a quote."

**Psychology:** They want price without diagnosis.

**Rebuttal:** "I can, but a quote without context can be misleading. Let me ask two questions so I do not send you something that looks cheap and fits badly."

## 9. "I do not want to be sold."

**Psychology:** They are bracing against pressure.

**Rebuttal:** "Fair. I do not need to sell you. I need to help you see if there is a gap worth addressing."
