# Recruiting Objections

## 1. "We are not hiring right now."

**Psychology:** They see recruiting as only useful when a role is already open.

**Rebuttal:** "Understood. The best recruiting conversations often happen before panic. Which role would hurt most if someone left?"

## 2. "We have internal recruiters."

**Psychology:** They are protecting internal ownership and budget.

**Rebuttal:** "Good. I am not here to replace them. Where do they need extra reach: hard-to-find profiles, speed, or confidential searches?"

## 3. "Your fee is too high."

**Psychology:** They are comparing fee to expense, not vacancy cost or bad-hire risk.

**Rebuttal:** "It is high if the role is easy to fill. What does the vacancy cost per month when the seat stays open?"

## 4. "We had a bad agency experience."

**Psychology:** Trust was damaged by poor screening or spammy candidate flow.

**Rebuttal:** "That would make me cautious too. Was the issue candidate quality, communication, speed, or understanding the role?"

## 5. "Just send candidates."

**Psychology:** They want value before giving time.

**Rebuttal:** "I can only send useful candidates after I understand the scorecard. Otherwise I am guessing and wasting your inbox."

## 6. "We can post the job ourselves."

**Psychology:** They assume applicant flow equals talent access.

**Rebuttal:** "You can. The question is whether the people you need are applying, or whether they have to be found and approached."

## 7. "We are not sure what we need yet."

**Psychology:** The role is still fuzzy, so hiring feels risky.

**Rebuttal:** "Then the first value is defining the profile, not sending resumes. What outcome must this hire own six months from now?"

## 8. "We need to compare agencies."

**Psychology:** They lack a clear reason to trust one partner.

**Rebuttal:** "Makes sense. What would make one firm clearly better: market access, speed, screening quality, or replacement guarantee?"

## 9. "The market is tight."

**Psychology:** They expect difficulty and may be lowering standards.

**Rebuttal:** "It is tight. That means the search needs sharper positioning, not lower standards. Why would the right person leave their current seat?"
