# SaaS Objections

## 1. "We already have a tool."

**Psychology:** The prospect is protecting status quo and avoiding migration pain.

**Rebuttal:** "Good. Then we do not need to debate tools in general. Where does the current setup still slow the team down?"

## 2. "Nobody used the last software."

**Psychology:** Adoption risk is larger than product interest.

**Rebuttal:** "That is the right concern. Software only matters if behavior changes. What made the last one hard to use?"

## 3. "It is too expensive."

**Psychology:** Value has not been tied to a measurable business leak.

**Rebuttal:** "That is fair if it only looks like another subscription. What is the current workaround costing in time, errors, or missed revenue?"

## 4. "We can build it internally."

**Psychology:** They want control or underestimate maintenance.

**Rebuttal:** "You might be able to. The decision is whether building and maintaining it is the best use of the team solving the original problem."

## 5. "Security will never approve this."

**Psychology:** They anticipate internal friction and want to avoid champion risk.

**Rebuttal:** "Then we should not skip that. What does security usually need first: data flow, compliance docs, access controls, or vendor review?"

## 6. "We are evaluating options."

**Psychology:** They lack decision criteria.

**Rebuttal:** "Makes sense. What are the two things the winning option has to prove?"

## 7. "Timing is bad."

**Psychology:** The pain is not urgent enough yet.

**Rebuttal:** "That may be true. What would have to happen for this to become a priority, and is any of that already starting?"

## 8. "Implementation sounds heavy."

**Psychology:** They fear disruption more than they want improvement.

**Rebuttal:** "Fair. A good solution should not create a second project bigger than the first problem. What implementation risk worries you most?"

## 9. "We need more stakeholders."

**Psychology:** The buyer needs consensus and does not want to carry the decision alone.

**Rebuttal:** "Good. Who would block this if they were not included early, and what would they need to see?"
