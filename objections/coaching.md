# Coaching & Consulting Objections

Industry-specific objections for coaches, consultants, and advisors.

---

## Trust & Proof

### "I don't believe coaching works."

**They're really worried about:** They've seen vague motivational advice sold as transformation.

**Your move:** "You should be skeptical. Good coaching isn't motivation — it should change decisions, behavior, and measurable outcomes. What would prove it worked for you?"

---

### "How do I know you actually deliver?"

**They're really worried about:** They'll pay and get nothing, or get fluff and platitudes.

**Your move:** "That's the right question. Here's how we structure it: [specific check-in, metrics, or money-back guarantee]. If we don't hit that, you're not paying."

---

### "I worked with a coach before and it didn't help."

**They're really worried about:** This is a scam or won't work for them.

**Your move:** "What didn't work? Was it the wrong fit, wrong timing, or the coach didn't give you something specific to do? Because that tells us if my approach is different."

---

## Cost & ROI

### "That's too much money."

**They're really worried about:** They can't justify the spend or the value isn't worth it.

**Your move:** "That's fair if it's just a cost. The question is what staying where you are is costing you. Most people are spending more in stress or lost opportunity than coaching costs."

---

### "I don't see the ROI."

**They're really worried about:** Coaching feels intangible. How do they measure success?

**Your move:** "Then we define what success looks like before we start. Not 'feel better.' Something measurable like [specific outcome]. If we don't hit it, we revisit the approach."

---

### "Can I get a refund if it doesn't work?"

**They're really worried about:** Risk. They need protection if this doesn't help.

**Your move:** "I offer a [time period] money-back guarantee if we're not hitting your goals. But that only works if we align on what those goals are upfront."

---

### "Your competitor is cheaper."

**They're really worried about:** Price comparison when all coaches look similar.

**Your move:** "They might be. Ask them: what's the biggest change you've seen a client make, and how fast? That's where the real difference shows up."

---

## Fit & Relevance

### "I'm not sure if this is for me."

**They're really worried about:** They don't fit the profile, or they're not ready yet.

**Your move:** "Let me ask: what brought you here? [Listen.] Based on what you're dealing with, this could be a fit or it might not. I'll tell you honestly after we talk more."

---

### "I'm not the decision-maker."

**They're really worried about:** They can't commit alone, or their boss won't approve it.

**Your move:** "Fair. Who is? And what would they need to hear for this to get approved? Sometimes it's not about coaching cost, it's about what outcome they care about."

---

### "My industry is different."

**They're really worried about:** Generic coaching won't work for their specific context.

**Your move:** "Maybe. Tell me about your industry. [Listen.] Based on what you described, here's how we'd tailor this. See if that lands."

---

## Timing & Readiness

### "I'm not ready yet."

**They're really worried about:** They know they should do something but feel unprepared.

**Your move:** "Usually the person who says 'I'm not ready' is exactly the person who needs help getting ready. So when would feel like the right time?"

---

### "Let me think about it."

**They're really worried about:** They're uncertain but don't want to say the real concern.

**Your move:** "Of course. Before you think on it, what's the part that's not clear: value, timing, whether this actually works, or something else? Let me answer that now."

---

### "We're not investing in training right now."

**They're really worried about:** Budget is locked, or coaching is a luxury, not essential.

**Your move:** "I hear that. The question is what the cost of staying where you are. Most of the time, that's bigger than coaching. But that's for you to decide."

---

## Results & Accountability

### "I don't have time for coaching."

**They're really worried about:** It'll be another thing on their to-do list they don't have bandwidth for.

**Your move:** "That's usually the people who need it most. But here's the truth: if you can't find 2-3 hours a month, this won't work. So either we make time or this isn't it."

---

### "I don't know what I want to work on."

**They're really worried about:** They're vague about their goals, so how can coaching help?

**Your move:** "That's actually what we figure out in the first session. We start with where you are, what's frustrating you most, and what would change the game. That becomes what we work on."

---

### "I want to see results first."

**They're really worried about:** They want proof before they pay.

**Your move:** "That's backwards. Results come from doing the work. But here's what I can do: [free session / trial / assessment]. That shows you how this actually works."

---

## Comparison & Alternatives

### "Can't I just read a book instead?"

**They're really worried about:** Coaching is expensive when books are cheap.

**Your move:** "You can. Most people read books and nothing changes because there's no accountability. Coaching is for when you want something to actually change."

---

### "I can get this from a mastermind group."

**They're really worried about:** Why pay 1-on-1 when group is cheaper?

**Your move:** "You can. Masterminds are great for ideas. Coaching is for when you need someone laser-focused on your specific situation. Different tools for different needs."

---

## Self-Doubt & Perfectionism

### "I'm afraid of failing in front of a coach."

**They're really worried about:** Vulnerability, judgment, or looking bad.

**Your move:** "That's the only way this works. Coaching is safe to fail because failure is data. We use it to adjust. If you're not risking failure, you're not growing."

---

### "I should be able to figure this out myself."

**They're really worried about:** They feel weak for needing help, or they should be independent.

**Your move:** "The best people have coaches. Athletes, executives, top performers. Not because they're weak — because they're serious about getting better. This is normal."

---

## Commitment & Long-Term

### "How long does coaching last?"

**They're really worried about:** They'll be locked in forever or dependent on you.

**Your move:** "We set a goal upfront — could be 3 months, 6 months, a year. Once you hit it, we're done. Some people come back later for new goals. Most move on and use what they learned."

---

### "I've already invested in [other program]."

**They're really worried about:** Doubling down feels wasteful, or they want to try the other thing first.

**Your move:** "How's that working? If you're getting traction, keep going. If you're stuck, that's where coaching helps. It's not either/or."

---

## Success Metrics

Coaching objections usually fall into:
- **Trust-based** (does it work, proof, skepticism) — solve with clarity on outcomes and accountability
- **Cost-based** (price, ROI, alternatives) — solve with value math and specific results
- **Readiness-based** (not ready, not the right time, unclear goals) — solve with honesty about timing and self-assessment
- **Commitment-based** (long-term, other investments, dependency) — solve with clear scope and exit strategy

Name which kind, then move tactically.
