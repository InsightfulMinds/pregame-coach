# Real Estate Objections

## 1. "The market is bad."

**Psychology:** Uncertainty is being used to delay decision-making.

**Rebuttal:** "The market is not a reason by itself. It changes the strategy. Are you trying to avoid a bad move, or avoid deciding?"

## 2. "We are just browsing."

**Psychology:** They want control and no pressure.

**Rebuttal:** "That is fine. Browsing gets useful when we define what would make a property worth action. What would have to be true?"

## 3. "Prices are too high."

**Psychology:** They are afraid of overpaying or buying at the wrong time.

**Rebuttal:** "That may be true in some areas. The question is not whether prices feel high; it is whether the right home at the right terms beats waiting."

## 4. "We want to wait for rates to drop."

**Psychology:** They are anchoring the whole decision to one variable.

**Rebuttal:** "Rates matter. So do buyer demand, price, and your timeline. If rates drop and everyone jumps back in, what changes for you?"

## 5. "We are not ready to list."

**Psychology:** The prep work feels bigger than the upside.

**Rebuttal:** "No problem. Then the useful move is not listing today. It is identifying what would need to be true for listing to make sense."

## 6. "Another agent promised a higher price."

**Psychology:** They are attracted to certainty, even if it is inflated.

**Rebuttal:** "A higher promise feels good. The real question is what the market will prove and what strategy supports that number."

## 7. "We do not want to be pressured."

**Psychology:** They fear losing control of a major life decision.

**Rebuttal:** "You should not feel pressured. My job is to give you enough clarity that you can move or not move with confidence."

## 8. "We can sell it ourselves."

**Psychology:** They want to protect equity and underestimate execution load.

**Rebuttal:** "You might. The question is whether the money saved is worth the pricing, negotiation, exposure, and legal risk you take on."

## 9. "We need to think about it."

**Psychology:** There is an unnamed risk blocking action.

**Rebuttal:** "Of course. Before you think, let's name the concern: price, timing, trust, or fear of making the wrong move?"
