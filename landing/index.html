Protocol

Seven steps. Five minutes. One call.

01

Mindset calibration

Name where you are at, not where you want to be. The coach adjusts from there.

02

Prospect read

Cold, warm, or referral. What they are likely guarding. How to open.

03

Objection preview

Top three objections, what they mean psychologically, and what to say that actually works.

04

Opening line

One specific line for this call. Not a script. A first sentence that earns the next one.

05

Confidence anchor

Not motivation. A frame. The thing you are actually here to do, so you do not drift.

06

Energy check + dial

Rate yourself 1–10. The coach adjusts. Then it sends you in.

What This Is Not

Less hype. More ready.

Not this

"Believe in yourself and your product. Energy is everything. Your enthusiasm will close deals."

Not this either

Generic talk tracks lifted from a blog post that your prospect has already heard twelve times this month.

This

A five-minute warmup for this call, this prospect, and the objection most likely to throw you off. Then you start steadier.

You do not need to feel fearless.

You need to know the first sentence, the likely objection, and the next clean question.
