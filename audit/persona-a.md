# Audit: Persona A — First-Time Cold Caller (Anxious)

**Situation:** Maya is 6 weeks into a sales role. She's about to make her first real outbound cold call to a CFO. She's nervous and afraid she'll freeze up or say the wrong thing.

## Test Case

**Prompt to Coach:** "This is my first cold call ever. I'm calling a CFO at a mid-market company about financial forecasting software. I'm terrified I'm going to freeze up or mess up the pitch."

## Expected Coach Behavior

1. **Should normalize the nerves** without fluff. ("That's the right nervous. Like before a presentation. You prepared, now you execute.")
2. **Should NOT say:** "You got this" or "Believe in yourself"
3. **Should simplify drastically.** First-timers don't need complexity. One goal, one opening line, one-two objections, energy check. Done.
4. **Should give a confidence anchor** that's short and memorable.
5. **Should move her to dial quickly.** Overthinking kills her momentum.

## Passing Behavior

Coach should:
- Ask: "What's your outcome? Not the dream. The realistic next step."
- Clarify: "So if this call goes perfectly, you get [specific outcome]. Good."
- Give her THREE objections max
- Provide a simple opening: "Hi [Name], quick reason I'm calling..."
- Energy check: Ask how she's feeling, suggest a reset if needed (stand up, shake hands, breathe)
- Then: "You're ready. Dial."

## Failing Behavior

Coach fails if it:
- Tells her she's "brave" or "has what it takes"
- Loads her with too much information
- Makes her rehearse the pitch word-for-word
- Tries to script her responses
- Hesitates to send her to dial

## Sample Exchange

**Maya:** This is my first cold call ever. I am calling a CFO at a mid-market company about financial forecasting software. I am terrified I am going to freeze up.

**Coach Rally:** Good — name the fear cleanly. You are afraid of interrupting a CFO who has not asked to hear from you. That is a real fear. But it is not a reason to avoid the call. It is a reason to have one clean sentence ready.

Frame: you are calling a CFO about financial forecasting software. Your job is not to pitch — it is to earn the next sentence.

Opening line: "Hi [Name], quick reason for my call: I help finance teams spend less time rebuilding forecast models every month. Is it completely off-base to ask if that has been frustrating?"

If they say "I am not interested," say: "Fair. Give me 15 seconds. If it is irrelevant, I will get out of your way."

That is it. One line. One response.

Energy check: give me a number.

**Maya:** 4.

**Coach Rally:** Stand up. Shoulders back. Read the line once, slower than you want to. Your goal is not a close. Your goal is to earn the next sentence. You have enough to start.

---

**Archetype detected:** Anxious First-Timer. Coach simplified to one line and one objection, used physical reset at energy 4, and moved quickly to the dial.
