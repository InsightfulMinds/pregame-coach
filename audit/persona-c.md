# Audit: Persona C — High-Volume Dialer (Speed)

**Situation:** Priya books 40+ calls a week for a commercial real estate firm. She's about to dial someone she's been trying to reach for 6 weeks. This is probably the last attempt before they go cold. She needs to be sharp and efficient.

## Test Case

**Prompt to Coach:** "I'm calling a developer I've been after for 6 weeks. This is my last shot before we move on. I need a quick pregame. I've got 30 more calls after this one. Make it fast."

## Expected Coach Behavior

1. **Should NOT bog her down.** High-volume dialers die from overthinking.
2. **Should give her the MINIMAL ritual.** 90 seconds max.
3. **Should reframe the "last shot."** ("If this is the last swing and nothing's changed, that's data. You move on. Don't hunt one person.")
4. **Should keep her in flow state.** Quick, clean, dial, next.
5. **Should not let urgency become desperation.**

## Passing Behavior

Coach should:
- Quick frame: "Who, what, when, why. Go."
- One objection max
- Opening line: "Say this naturally."
- Energy check: "You in flow or tired?"
- Send her: "Dial. If he doesn't pick, one voicemail, next."
- Should acknowledge: "This can't be the one. The one is whoever says yes."

## Failing Behavior

Coach fails if it:
- Loads her with five objections
- Makes her rehearse
- Makes her feel like this call is do-or-die
- Tries to deepen her strategy when she needs speed
- Doesn't respect that she has 30 more calls today

## Sample Exchange

**Priya:** Calling a developer I have been after for 6 weeks. Last attempt. I have 30 more calls after this. Make it fast.

**Coach Rally:** Quick frame: commercial real estate developer, cold after 6 weeks, last dial.

Opening line: "Hey, I have been trying to reach you and I do not want to be the person who keeps calling without a reason. Quick question: is commercial space still on your radar, or should I stop reaching out?"

That gives them a clean exit. If they stay, you earned the next 30 seconds honestly.

If they say "not interested," say: "Fair. If anything changes on the space side, I will be easy to find." Then hang up and move on.

Anchor: "This is not the one. The one is whoever says yes."

**Priya:** Got it. Dialing.

**Coach Rally:** Good. At call 30, check whether your opener still sounds fresh. If it does not, rewrite it in 10 seconds. Go.

---

**Archetype detected:** High-Volume Dialer. Coach matched her pace, gave one line and one response, skipped energy check, and kept the exchange under 60 seconds.
