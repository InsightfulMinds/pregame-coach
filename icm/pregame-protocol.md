# Pregame Protocol

Use this protocol before a cold call, warm follow-up, referral appointment, demo, consultation, or close.

## 1. Call Frame

Lock the facts in one sentence:

```text
I am calling a [prospect type] [buyer/persona] in [industry] about [product/service] to create [specific next step].
```

If the user cannot name the next step, make them choose one: book a meeting, confirm fit, handle one objection, ask for the close, or earn permission to follow up.

## 2. Mindset Calibration

Ask:

```text
What are you trying to protect right now: your ego, your commission, or the prospect's clarity?
```

Then reset:

```text
Your job is not to be liked. Your job is to create a clean decision.
```

## 3. Prospect Read

Name the prospect's likely state before the user speaks.

- Cold prospect: interrupted, skeptical, looking for a reason to exit.
- Warm prospect: curious but not yet convinced the problem is urgent.
- Referral: more open, but still protecting trust and time.

The coach should say what the prospect is probably afraid of, not just what they might say.

## 4. Objection Preview

Select the top three objections. For each:

- Say the objection.
- Translate the psychology.
- Give a conversational rebuttal in one or two sentences.

No monologues. No courtroom closing arguments.

## 5. Confidence Anchor

Build one short line the user can return to if nerves spike:

```text
I am here to create clarity, not force a yes.
```

The anchor must be repeatable under stress.

## 6. Opening Line Prep

Write one opening line that sounds spoken, not scripted.

Pattern:

```text
Hey [name], quick reason for my call: I help [specific group] solve [specific pain] without [common fear]. Is it completely off-base to ask if that is on your radar?
```

For warm or referral calls, replace interruption language with context:

```text
[Referrer/context] pointed me your way because [likely pain]. I want to see if there is a real fit before we waste time.
```

## 7. Energy Check

Ask for an energy number from 1 to 10.

- 1-5: stand up, shoulders back, one slow breath, read the opening line once.
- 6-7: simplify the first sentence and call.
- 8-10: keep the pace down; do not outspeed the prospect.

End with the dial instruction. Preparation is only useful if it becomes action.
