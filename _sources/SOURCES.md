# Sources

The Pregame Coach methodology draws from the following frameworks and literature.

## Sales Psychology

1. **Cialdini, Robert** — *Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion* — foundational work on reciprocity, social proof, commitment, and scarcity as decision drivers; informs objection psychology classifications
2. **Carnegie, Dale** — *How to Win Friends and Influence People* — rapport and listening frameworks; used as a contrast baseline (the pregame de-emphasizes relationship-first rapport in favor of relevance-first opening)
3. **Voss, Chris** — *Never Split the Difference* — tactical empathy, mirroring, and labeling techniques; directly informs objection rebuttal design (name the emotion before countering)

## Objection Handling

4. **Cardone, Grant** — *Sell or Be Sold* — high-volume cold call mindset; used only as contrast for action-orientation, softened here into preparation-first language
5. **Belfort, Jordan** — Straight Line Persuasion System — tone certainty, energy calibration, and three-yes structure; objection playbook structure adapted from SLP frameworks (manipulative tactics excluded)
6. **Keating, Neil** Industry sales training literature — compliance, objection cadence patterns in insurance and real estate verticals

## Pre-Performance Psychology

7. **Nideffer, Robert** — attentional focus and arousal theory — foundation for the energy calibration step (1-10 rating) and physical state anchors before high-stakes calls
8. **Seligman, Martin** — learned helplessness and explanatory style — informs slump diagnosis: separating temporary setbacks from pattern-level beliefs about ability
9. **Csikszentmihalyi, Mihaly** — *Flow* — optimal challenge/skill balance; informs coaching judgment on when a rep is under-calibrated (anxious) vs. over-calibrated (complacent)

## Coaching Methodology

10. **Gallwey, Timothy** — *The Inner Game of Tennis* — self-interference and inner critic suppression; directly informs the "do not shame the user for nerves" rule
11. **Whitmore, John** — *Coaching for Performance* — GROW model foundations; session arc (Goal, Reality, Options, Will) adapted into pregame structure
12. **Rock, David** — SCARF model (Status, Certainty, Autonomy, Relatedness, Fairness) — maps to prospect threat-response in objection psychology classifications
